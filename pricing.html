Frequently Asked Questions

How do I subscribe?

Download UniPlan for your platform and create an account. You can subscribe directly inside the app with just a few taps.

Can I switch plans?

Yes! You can upgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately.

Is there a free trial?

No, but premium features are available immediately upon subscribing. The free version includes all core features with no time limit.

How do payments work?

Payments are processed securely through the App Store or Google Play. Your subscription auto-renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled through the App Store or Google Play or Paddle depending on your platform. Please contact their support for refund requests.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. Cloud Sync data is encrypted end-to-end. We never share your data with third parties. See our Privacy Policy for details.
